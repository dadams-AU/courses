  1. Change things a little at a time
  2. Avoid the spotlight

Paper 2

Is “deservingness” an explanation, or a label for the outcome?

The prompt, in plain terms

Deservingness: the idea that some people have earned help (workers, children, the old, the disabled) and others have not

Pick one:

  • It explains. Then it predicts something. Say what, and test it on a case.
  • It doesn’t. Then something else does the work. Name it.

The readings for Paper 2

  • Howard, ch. 5
  • Campbell, chs. 1–3
  • Desmond, ch. 5
