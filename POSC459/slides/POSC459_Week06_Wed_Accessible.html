Three rules before you start

  • Hypothetical household only. Never your own information
  • Never enter a name, address, phone, email, or SSN
  • Never start an application. These are screeners

A site asks for personal information? Stop. Screenshot that page and use it.

Step 1 · IRS EITC Assistant

apps.irs.gov/app/eitc

  1. Tax year: 2025
  2. Answer every question as your household
  3. Not on your household card? Take the simple answer: no
  4. Screenshot the result page

About 10 minutes. No login, no personal information.

Step 2 · CalFresh screener

benefitscal.com → Ask Robin → See if I qualify

  1. County: Orange
  2. Household size: A = 3 · B = 3 · C = 1
  3. Income: the monthly figure on your card
  4. People working: 1
  5. Screenshot the result

About 5 minutes. Robin says whether you may qualify, not how much.

While you click, keep a list

  • Every question each screener asked
  • The one that decided your result
  • One it never asked that you expected

Step 3 · Write 150–250 words

  1. What does your household get from each program?
  2. Which question decided the most? What does it assume about who deserves help?
  3. What did a screener not ask, and why might that matter?

Name one reading if it fits (Howard, Campbell, or Desmond, with a page).
