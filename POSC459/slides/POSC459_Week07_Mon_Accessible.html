Food: charity and business are in the net

  • Feeding America: 200 food banks, 60,000 pantries and kitchens
  • 40 million people, about 100 meals each a year
  • Walmart: $11 billion in SNAP sales (2018)
  • 250,000 retailers take SNAP

Howard 2023, ch. 6

Food: is it enough?

14.3 million households food insecure (2018): one in nine

  • Most of them are above the poverty line
  • 44% get nothing from SNAP, WIC, or school meals
  • Black households 21.2% · Hispanic 16.2% · White 8.1%

Howard 2023, ch. 6, table 6.1
